# `GermanCommonTypos` takes every word here, types it the ways fingers slip
# (one letter left out, two letters swapped, one letter struck twice) and

# *Beisiel* cuts into *Bei* + *Siel*, *Zusand* into *zu* + *Sand*.
# One word per line, as it is written; `#` starts a comment.
